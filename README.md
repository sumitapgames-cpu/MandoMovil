# Mando Móvil

**Your phone is the controller.** Swing it like a paddle, turn it like a steering wheel. No gamepad needed.

Mando Móvil is a party game prototype. One device shows the game (PC or Android) and every phone on the same Wi-Fi becomes a motion controller.

## Download

Get the latest version from the [Releases](../../releases) page:

- `MandoMovil.exe`: Windows (64-bit)
- `MandoMovil.apk`: Android

## Games

- **Ping pong**: play against the AI or 1 vs 1 with a friend. Swing your phone to hit forehands, backhands and lobs.
- **Racing**: hold your phone sideways and steer like a wheel. Free drive, rally stage and a timed lap on a city circuit.
- **Baseball**: swing your phone like a bat. Easy, normal, hard and versus.
- **Boxing**: punch the bag.

## How to play

You need one **host** (the screen everyone watches) and one or more **controllers** (the phones you play with).

- **Host**: a Windows PC (`MandoMovil.exe`) or an Android phone/tablet (`MandoMovil.apk`). Tip: cast or plug an Android host into a TV for a console-like setup.
- **Controllers**: Android phones with `MandoMovil.apk`.

1. Connect the host and all phones to the **same Wi-Fi network**.
2. Open the game on the host, choose **Host** and pick a game.
3. Open the app on each phone, choose **Controller** and join the room.
4. Hold your phone, press **Center** when asked, and play.

## Install notes

- **Windows**: it may show "Windows protected your PC" because the exe isn't signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Android**: allow installing apps from unknown sources when prompted.
- If the phones can't find the host, allow the exe through the Windows Firewall (private networks).
- iPhone is not supported yet.
